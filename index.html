Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

01 Overview

At Pod, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your data to provide a live, real-time experience for you and your friend group. By using the app, you consent to the practices described here.

02 Data Collection & Usage

Precise Location Data

We collect GPS coordinates to enable real-time map features and pod tracking. This includes Background Location access, allowing your pod members to see your location even when the app is minimized.

Account Identifiers

We use Firebase Authentication to collect your email address and name. This is required to sync your pods and identify you to the friends in them.

User Generated Content

Plans, schedules, notes, and favorite locations you save are stored in our Firebase Realtime Database to ensure all pod members have the latest details.

03 Data Sharing

Your data is private. We do not sell your personal information. Your precise location, schedule, and pod updates are only shared with users you have explicitly authorized by joining a shared "Pod." You can block any user, which immediately stops all location and content sharing with them. We utilize Google Firebase for secure data infrastructure.

04 Data Control & Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated pod data at any time via the in-app settings menu. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our Firebase production servers.

Location & Battery

Why does Pod need my location in the background? +

Pod shows your pod members where you are in real time, similar to how a live location app works. Background location lets that stay accurate even when you're not actively looking at the app, so your friends can see you're on the way without you having to keep Pod open. You can turn this off any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Pod.

Will Pod drain my battery? +

Pod adjusts how often it updates your location based on how you're moving. When you're stationary, it checks in far less often; when you're actively traveling, it updates more frequently to keep the map accurate. This keeps battery impact low compared to apps that ping location at a fixed interval regardless of movement.

Can I hide my location from specific people? +

Yes. You can block any user from your profile or a pod's member list. Blocking immediately stops all location and content sharing between you and that person in both directions.

Pods & Plans

What's a pod? +

A pod is a persistent group for your friends, not a one-off event. Once you create a pod, it stays as your shared home base: a live map, a shared feed, and everyone's schedules.

How do I invite friends to my pod? +

Open your pod and generate an invite code from the members screen. Share it however you'd normally send a link or code, anyone who enters it joins the pod immediately and appears on the shared map.

Can I keep my schedule private? +

Yes. Schedule blocks can be marked public or private. Public blocks are visible to your pod so they know when you're free; private blocks stay visible only to you.

My plan isn't saving, what's wrong? +

When adding a location to a plan, make sure you select an actual result from the address dropdown rather than typing free text and dismissing it. The correct location shows small gray text underneath it confirming a real place was selected before you save.

Account & Privacy

How do I delete my account? +

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your profile, location history, and pod content from Pod's servers. This action can't be undone.

Who can see my information? +

Only people in a pod with you can see your location, schedule, and plans within that pod. Pod does not sell your data. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Someone is bothering me in a pod, what do I do? +

Block them directly from their profile or the pod's member list, this cuts off all sharing between you two immediately. If it's a safety concern, email us and include the pod name and the person's username so we can look into it.

01 Why I built Pod

My friend group was (and is) genuinely crap at planning things. We'd try to organize something and Elijah would get mad, Jay wouldn't read the chat, Rafe + Luis would be dead asleep, Davis would be halfway across town, and who knows if Ali was even alive. Somehow I was still worse than all of them combined.

The real problem wasn't that they didn't want to come. It was that the plan lived in a wall of text nobody was going to scroll through, and there was nowhere for the group to just exist between plans. So I thought, what if the group had a permanent home, not just a chat that resets to zero context every time?

That's Pod. You open the app, you see where everyone is, what they're up to, and what's coming up. No group chat archaeology. You show up.

02 Background

I'm a CS student at the University of Florida, graduating December 2026. Outside of Pod, I do machine learning research in genomics and automation engineering at UF.

Building Pod taught me more than I expected — Firebase security rules that silently fail at 2am, animated marker bugs that only show up with 4+ users, background location on iOS being its own beast entirely. The kind of stuff you only learn by shipping something real.

That kind of freedom to build things that matter is what I'm after.
